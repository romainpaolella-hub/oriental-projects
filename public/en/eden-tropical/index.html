Lipa Noi · The island, differently

The island's hidden gem

Lipa Noi, on the west coast of Koh Samui, offers a preserved island experience: the beach 250 m away, an ultra-quiet, protected setting, perfect for lovers of sunsets over the Andaman Sea.

Each villa blends the Balinese spirit with contemporary comfort — open volumes, natural materials, tinted polished concrete and raw wood create a zen atmosphere turned towards the garden and the pool.
