Frequently asked questions

Investor FAQ

Can a foreigner buy a villa in Thailand?

A non-resident cannot hold freehold title to the land in their own name, but can secure use of the villa through a renewable long-term lease (leasehold), or via a Thai company structure. The arrangement is validated case by case by an independent firm.

What does the listed price cover?

The construction of the villa, its private pool and garden. The land lease, maintenance charges, transfer fees and taxes are billed separately.

Who handles the rental?

Our local team can handle marketing, guest reception, housekeeping and maintenance, with income and expense reporting.

General information provided for guidance only. It does not constitute legal, tax or financial advice and should be validated with a Thai lawyer or notary before any commitment.
