Lease & buy-back

30-year land lease, with a buy-back option

The villa's price includes a 30-year land lease, with an option to buy back the land — an alternative to freehold ownership, which is not accessible to non-residents in Thailand. To go further in an approach that is both economical and responsible, solar panels can be offered as an option, reducing electricity consumption and supporting more sustainable management of the property.

Frequently asked questions

Investor FAQ

Can a foreigner buy a villa in Thailand?

A non-resident cannot hold freehold title to the land in their own name, but can secure the use of the villa through a long-term lease (leasehold) with a buy-back option, as is the case for Palm Sea View. The structure is validated case by case by an independent law firm.

What does the listed price cover?

The construction of the villa, its private infinity pool, its 800 sqm plot and the 30-year lease with buy-back option. Solar panels, transfer fees and taxes are billed separately.

Who manages the rental?

Our local team can handle marketing, guest welcome, housekeeping and maintenance, with reporting on income and costs.

Are solar panels included?

No — they are offered as an option, to reduce electricity consumption and support more sustainable management of the property.

Can the villa be customised?

Customisation options (finishes, kitchen, bathroom) are detailed in the contractual documents provided at reservation.
