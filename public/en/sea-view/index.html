The story · Villa Sea View

There are places where the landscape becomes part of the house

Villa Sea View is a private residence of four contemporary villas, set within a coconut grove and open to the sea, in Ban Tai, a few minutes from the beach. The residence enjoys a preserved tropical setting where the vegetation and the ocean horizon form a naturally exceptional backdrop.

Each villa has been designed around this privileged relationship with its surroundings. Private pool, open terraces and through-flowing living spaces extend the interior naturally towards the grove and the sea.
