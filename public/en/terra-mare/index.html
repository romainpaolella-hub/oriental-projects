Bophut · The island's historic address

The liveliest neighbourhood in Koh Samui

Bophut is one of the most sought-after areas on the north coast: its restored old fishing village, the Fisherman's Village, brings together restaurants, galleries and a night market a few minutes from the residence.

Each Terra Mare villa has its own pool, a covered outdoor kitchen and a private garden — a contemporary setting designed for entertaining as much as for unwinding.
