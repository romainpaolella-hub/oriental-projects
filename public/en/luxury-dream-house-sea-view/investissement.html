Lease & buy-back

Why leasehold, for this type of residence

The purchase price of Luxury Dream House Sea View includes a 30-year land lease, offering long-term security and peace of mind, along with an option to buy back the land, subject to applicable conditions. Unlike a Thai Limited Company structure, leasehold requires no ongoing administrative management or annual corporate accounting — a simpler alternative for a buyer who prioritises clarity of structure over indirect land ownership.

Precise buy-back option terms (duration, conditions, price) provided with the full file.

Frequently asked questions

Investor FAQ

Why is this residence sold on leasehold rather than through a Thai company?

Both structures allow a non-resident to secure the use of a property in Thailand. Leasehold, chosen here, offers a simple and predictable framework — a 30-year lease with a land buy-back option — without the management and accounting obligations of a company. It is also the structure the seller wants to test for this type of high-end residence.

What does the listed price cover?

The construction of the residence, delivered fully furnished and equipped, its 4 × 12 m pool, its 1,411 sqm plot and the 30-year lease with buy-back option. Transfer fees and taxes are billed separately.

What happens at the end of the 30 years?

The land buy-back option, its terms and its price are detailed in the contractual documents, provided on request before any decision.

Who manages the rental if I want to let the residence?

Our local team can handle marketing, guest welcome, housekeeping and maintenance, with reporting on income and costs — as with the other properties in the portfolio.

Can the plot be visited before purchase?

Yes, a visit to the plot on site, or a detailed presentation of the project (plans, artist's impressions) by video call, can be arranged with our local French-speaking team.
