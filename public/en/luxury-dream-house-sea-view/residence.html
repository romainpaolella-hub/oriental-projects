An exclusive villa, an exceptional panorama

The residence features four bedrooms and four bathrooms, including two luxurious master suites with sea and pool views, each with a private shower and bathtub. Two additional bedrooms each offer their own unique view, of the sea or the garden. This exceptional private residence is fully furnished and equipped, ready to move in and enjoy from day one.

Outside, a spectacular 4 × 12 m pool accompanies a large outdoor sala, creating an elegant setting for relaxation and entertaining. A generous 1,411 sqm plot and a rare view, private parking for two vehicles and, above all, the exclusivity of a private residence not part of a residential development.
