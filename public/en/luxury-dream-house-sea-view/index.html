Space, tranquillity and privacy

With 530 sqm of total construction on a particularly generous 1,411 sqm plot, the residence offers a rare sense of space, tranquillity and privacy. Two master suites enjoy a direct view of the sea and the pool, each with a private shower and bathtub; two additional bedrooms each have their own view, of the sea or the garden.

Outside, a spectacular 4 × 12 m pool accompanies a large outdoor sala, creating an elegant setting for relaxation and entertaining — a generous plot, private parking for two vehicles and, above all, the exclusivity of a private residence not part of a residential development.
