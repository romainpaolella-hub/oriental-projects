Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

1. Data controller

This site is published by Oriental Projects Promotion, a property developer in Koh Samui, Thailand. For any question about your data: contact@orientalprojects.co.

2. Data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily enter in the "Receive the brochure" form: full name, email address, and — if you provide them — phone number, country of residence, the programme you're interested in, your project intention and a free-text message. No account, no advertising profiling, no marketing trackers.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • To email you the requested brochure and information about the chosen programme.
  • To follow up with you about your request (by email, phone or WhatsApp).

The legal basis is your consent, collected via the form's checkbox, together with pre-contractual steps taken at your request.

4. Recipients and processors

Your data is processed only by the Oriental Projects team and by the technical providers strictly necessary:

  • Netlify — website hosting and receipt of form submissions.
  • Resend — the service that sends the email containing your brochure.

These providers may host data outside the European Union; they provide appropriate contractual safeguards. Your data is never sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

5. Retention period

Requests are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact, then deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise them, write to contact@orientalprojects.co. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (in France, the CNIL).

7. Cookies and third-party services

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Two external resources are loaded for display: Google Fonts and, on some pages, an embedded Google Maps map. Loading them sends your IP address to Google. The map is only loaded on the "Location" and "Contact" pages.

8. Contact

A question about this policy or about your data? contact@orientalprojects.co or WhatsApp at +66 655 767 871.
