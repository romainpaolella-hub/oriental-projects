The developer

18 years creating places that belong to Koh Samui

For 18 years, Oriental Projects has been developing private residences in Koh Samui, guided by a simple conviction: the finest properties are those that sit naturally within their surroundings.

Each residence begins with close attention to the site, the light, the volumes and the dialogue between architecture and landscape. From design to construction, every step is considered to create places that are lasting, elegant and deeply rooted in the island's identity.

A long-term vision, for those who choose Koh Samui to live, pass on or invest.
